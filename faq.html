Ski Club FAQ

Answers to common questions about West Middle School Ski Club.

When is ski club?

Our designated ski club day is Thursday, from 4:30 p.m. until closing, but you’re welcome to arrive earlier. Your punch card is valid every day during the season, so you can enjoy Timber Ridge beyond ski club Thursdays!

Is transportation provided to and from Timber Ridge?

No, families are responsible for transportation to and from Timber Ridge. A parent, guardian, or trusted family friend must remain at Timber Ridge with your child.

I’ll usually be there on Thursdays, our designated ski club day, but I may not be there every week.

Can I use my punch card any day?

Yes! You can use your punch card any day during the season—not just Thursdays. Adult lift ticket pricing varies on weekends and holidays. Please see the signup page for pricing details.

How do lessons work?

Students ages 7 and up can take two beginner lessons through Timber Ridge Ski School for $10 per lesson. These discounted lessons are available on Thursdays, our designated club day, during the first four weeks students attend.

Contact Ski School directly at 269-694-9449, ext. 122 to schedule your lessons.

Are chaperone passes available?

If you’ve been part of ski club in the past, you may remember chaperone passes, which provide an adult with a free lift ticket and ski rentals. Depending on how many students sign up, I expect to have some available again this season.

Email me when you plan to visit, and I’ll do my best to spread the passes among interested families. Availability is limited, and the rules can change from year to year. I’m still confirming this season’s details and will keep everyone posted.

Still have a question? Contact Henry at Henrylumbard@gmail.com.
